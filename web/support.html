Help with the FRSH Nearby app for customers and producers.

Need a hand? Email us and we will get back to you, usually within two working days. Please include the email you signed up with and, if it is about an order, the farm and the date.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete account, read the notice, type DELETE and confirm. Your personal details are removed and you can no longer sign in. Orders that are still in progress are cancelled, and finished orders stay in the records without your name, email or phone number. This cannot be undone.

How do I report a product, a farm or a person?

Use Report in the ⋮ menu of a chat or product, or in Settings. Tell us what is wrong. Our team reads every report and can hide content or remove accounts that break the rules.

How do I block someone, and undo it?

Open the chat with them, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Block. Neither of you can then message or order from the other. To undo it, go to Settings → Account → Blocked people and tap Unblock.

How do I cancel an order?

Open Orders, choose the order and tap Cancel. You can cancel until the order is completed. If the cancel button is gone, message the farm in the order chat or write to us.

My order has not been accepted or picked up.

Producers accept orders themselves. Message the farm from the order chat. If you cannot reach them, email us with the order details and we will help.

I cannot sign in.
  • Use the same method you signed up with: email and password, Google, or Apple.
  • Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen to reset it by email.
  • If you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, sign in with Apple again rather than with an email address.

Still stuck? Email us from the address you registered with.

How do I become a producer?

When you sign up, choose a producer account type instead of, or in addition to, a customer account, and fill in your farm or business details. Our team may ask for documents to confirm who you are. You will be notified when you are approved.

I am not getting notifications.

Check that notifications for FRSH Nearby are allowed in your phone's settings, and that they are switched on in the app's Settings. Then close and reopen the app.

What data do you keep about me?

See our Privacy Policy. To get a copy of your data or have it corrected, email us.
